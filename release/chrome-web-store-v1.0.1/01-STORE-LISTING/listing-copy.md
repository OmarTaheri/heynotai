## Name

heynotai — AI Content Detector

## Summary

Detect likely AI-generated text, images, and videos while you browse, with clear confidence scores and saved scan history.

## Category

Productivity

## Language

English

## Detailed description

heynotai helps you check text, images, and videos for signs of AI generation without leaving the page.

Use the extension to:

• Scan selected text from the right-click menu

• Analyze supported images and video pages

• See likely human, likely AI, or mixed results with confidence scores

• Open a browser drawer for recent scans and model details

• Sign in with your heynotai website or Google account

• Keep scan preferences synchronized across the website and extension

AI detection is probabilistic. A result is evidence to consider, not proof of authorship or authenticity. Important decisions should include human review and other evidence.

A heynotai account and an internet connection to the heynotai API are required. Audio detection is not currently available.
